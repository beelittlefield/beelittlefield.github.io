Bee Littlefield writes contemporary cozy mystery stories featuring relatable, messy characters and lots of coffee.

Want to read some of Bee's books for free? Here's how:

  2. If you're up for a little detective work of your own, you can also get a free copy of Book 1, Clueless at the Coffee Station. There are tiny clues scattered all over the main pages of this website. Collect them all and decode the secret ten-letter word or phrase. E-mail it to Bee@BeeLittlefield.com and you'll get a code for the free e-book. (You do not have to sign up for the newsletter for this.) Happy sleuthing!
